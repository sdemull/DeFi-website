Enrollment Steps

How to enroll in 5 steps.

The online enrollment process takes about 10 minutes. Here’s exactly what to expect.

1
Go to the enrollment portal
Visit capitalgroup.com/myplan/e5rww — this link is specific to the Advanced Farm Equipment plan.
2
Create or log in to your account
New participants will create a username and password. You’ll need your Social Security number and date of birth to verify your identity.
3
Choose your contribution rate
Decide what percentage of your paycheck to contribute. You can always change this later. Contributing enough to capture any employer match is the first priority.
4
Select your investments
Choose from the fund lineup below, or select a Target Date Fund that automatically adjusts as you approach retirement. Not sure which to pick? Reach out to Sage.
5
Name your beneficiaries
Designate who should receive your account if something happens to you. This step is often skipped — don’t skip it.
⚠  Share Class Change — Effective July 1st
All investment options in the plan will transition from R-2 share classes to R-5E share classes effective July 1st. R-5E shares carry lower expense ratios, which means more of your investment return stays in your account. No action is required on your part — your existing balances and elections will transfer automatically.

Personal Financial Planning

Get a clearer picture of your financial future.

As an employee of Advanced Farm Equipment, you have access to set up a complimentary RightCapital account through DeMull Financial. RightCapital is a financial planning tool that lets you see your full financial picture — retirement projections, net worth, cash flow, and more.

To get the most out of your account, we’ll want to gather the following information from you:

Income & Employment
Current salary or hourly wage • Spouse/partner income • Any other income sources • Current 401(k) contribution rate
Assets & Accounts
Checking & savings balances • Other retirement accounts (IRA, old 401k) • Investment accounts • Real estate equity
Debts & Obligations
Mortgage balance & payment • Car loans • Student loans • Credit card balances • Monthly minimum payments
Goals & Timeline
Target retirement age • Monthly spending in retirement • Major upcoming expenses • Protection needs (life, disability)
