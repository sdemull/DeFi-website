Protect yourself. Benefit yourself.

As a plan sponsor, you carry legal responsibility for your company’s 401(k). We serve as your co-fiduciary — helping you manage the plan correctly, protect yourself from liability, and maximize the plan’s benefit to you as the owner.

Two Problems We Solve

Most plan sponsors are exposed. Most plans aren’t optimized for the owner.

Our Advisory Services

Full-service plan sponsor support, from fiduciary documentation to owner optimization.

Co-Fiduciary Services
As your 3(38) or 3(21) investment fiduciary, we formally share responsibility for investment decisions — reducing your personal liability and ensuring the plan meets ERISA standards for prudent management.
Investment Policy Statement
An IPS is the foundation of a defensible fiduciary process. We create, maintain, and ensure you’re following a current IPS — which is the first thing a DOL auditor will ask for.
Investment Lineup Review
Quarterly reviews of your plan’s investment options against benchmarks, with documented meeting minutes. We identify underperforming or high-cost funds and recommend replacements when warranted.
Fee Benchmarking
ERISA requires plan sponsors to ensure fees are reasonable. We benchmark your plan’s total cost against comparable plans annually and document the analysis — the evidence you need if you’re ever audited.
Owner Benefit Optimization
We design your plan using profit sharing, comparability testing, and Safe Harbor provisions to maximize what you can contribute as the owner — while keeping the required employee contribution as low as legally possible.
Employee Education & Enrollment
High employee participation rates improve your plan’s nondiscrimination test results — which allows you as the owner to contribute more. We provide enrollment meetings and ongoing education to drive participation.

Common Questions

Questions I get asked most

What does a 401(k) plan advisor actually do?

A plan advisor handles investment selection and monitoring, benchmarks plan fees, supports the fiduciary documentation process, advises on plan design such as Safe Harbor and profit sharing, and delivers employee enrollment and education meetings. The advisory role is separate from the recordkeeper and third-party administrator.

How much does 401(k) advisory cost?

DeMull Financial charges a tiered asset-based fee for plan advisory services starting at 0.75% and declining with plan size, subject to a $3,500 annual minimum. Fees are disclosed in writing and are separate from recordkeeping and administration costs charged by the plan provider.

Why does employee participation matter to the business owner?

Nondiscrimination testing compares deferrals by owners and highly compensated employees against everyone else. When rank-and-file participation is low, the owner's own contributions get capped or refunded. Improving employee participation directly raises what the owner is allowed to defer, which is why plan education is not just an HR benefit.
