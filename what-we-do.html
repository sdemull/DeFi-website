Our Services

Six areas. One integrated plan.

Each service below is a standalone capability. For most clients, two or more work together — because that’s where the real value is.

Financial Planning
A written, comprehensive financial plan that connects every area of your financial life — retirement, tax, cash flow, protection, and estate — into one coordinated strategy. Built in RightCapital and reviewed with you regularly.

Investment Management

Tax Planning
Proactive, year-round tax strategy for business owners and self-employed professionals. S-Corp elections, retirement deductions, Roth conversions, QBI optimization — coordinated with your CPA, not duplicating their work.

04
Retirement Plan Design
Custom retirement plan design that maximizes what goes into your pocket — Solo 401(k), Safe Harbor, Defined Benefit, SEP-IRA. Built using comparability testing and profit sharing to benefit you as the owner, not just your employees.

Business Owner Planning
Integrated planning that connects your business and personal finances — entity structure, owner compensation, cash strategy, exit planning, and protection. We sit at the intersection of both worlds and make sure every decision in one optimizes the other.

Plan Sponsor Advisory
Co-fiduciary 401(k) advisory for business owners who offer a retirement plan. Fee benchmarking, investment policy statements, quarterly reviews, and plan design that maximizes your personal contribution — while protecting you from ERISA liability.

How We Work

Three principles behind everything we do.

These aren’t marketing claims. They describe how we actually operate.

Fiduciary, Always
We are legally obligated to act in your best interest — not ours, not a product provider's. That standard shapes every recommendation we make.
Integrated, Not Siloed
Tax, investments, retirement, protection — these aren't separate conversations. They're one strategy. Everything we do connects to everything else.
Proactive, Not Reactive
We don't wait for you to call with a problem. We're watching for opportunities and flagging issues before they become decisions you have to make under pressure.
