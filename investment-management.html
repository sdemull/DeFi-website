What We Do

Evidence-based investing.
Coordinated with your plan.

Your investment portfolio shouldn’t exist in isolation. We manage your assets in direct coordination with your tax strategy, retirement timeline, and financial plan — so every dollar is working toward the same goal.

Our Philosophy

We don’t chase returns. We build portfolios that survive the full market cycle.

Evidence-based investing means building diversified, low-cost portfolios grounded in decades of academic research — not market predictions or gut feelings. We use institutional-quality investment vehicles to give you professional-grade exposure without the institutional price tag.

Every portfolio we build is personalized to your risk tolerance, time horizon, tax situation, and goals — and managed on an ongoing basis as those factors evolve.

Low-cost, diversified portfolios using institutional-quality funds
Risk tolerance and time horizon matched to your specific goals
Tax-coordinated — managed alongside your tax planning, not separately
Regular rebalancing to maintain your target allocation
Tax-loss harvesting to reduce drag on your returns
Transparent reporting — you always know exactly what you own and why
What We Manage

Every account, working together.

We look at your full investment picture — not just the accounts we manage — and make sure everything is coordinated.

Personal Investment Accounts
Taxable brokerage accounts managed for long-term growth and tax efficiency. Asset location strategy ensures the right investments are in the right accounts.
IRA & Roth IRA
Traditional and Roth IRAs managed with a long-term tax strategy in mind — including ongoing Roth conversion analysis based on your income and future tax projections.
401(k) & Business Retirement Plans
We advise on your employer plan investments and design business retirement plans to maximize what goes into your pocket through appropriate fund selection and contribution strategy.
Business-Held Assets
For business owners with cash sitting in their company, we evaluate the most tax-efficient way to deploy those assets — whether personally, inside the business, or through a retirement plan.
Rollover & Consolidation
Old 401(k)s from previous employers, inherited IRAs, and scattered accounts consolidated into a cohesive strategy. Fewer accounts means less complexity and better coordination.
Ongoing Portfolio Monitoring
We actively monitor your portfolio for drift, tax-loss harvesting opportunities, and rebalancing triggers — so you don’t have to watch the market to know your investments are on track.
