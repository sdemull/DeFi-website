Contractors, electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs, and skilled tradespeople face a financial reality most advisors don’t understand — irregular income, equipment costs, and no employer taking care of retirement.

We do. And we build plans around how your business actually works — not some textbook version of it.

Built for the Trades

Irregular income isn’t a problem to work around — it’s the reality we plan for.

We build systems that work whether you’re in your busy season or your slow one, so you can stop white-knuckling it every January.

What We Do For You

Planning that works around your business, not against it

Cash Flow Planning
We help you build a system for managing irregular income — covering lean months, setting aside taxes, and still building wealth consistently.
Self-Employed Retirement
Solo 401(k), SEP-IRA, SIMPLE IRA — we find the right structure and help you contribute as aggressively as possible while minimizing your tax bill.
Business Structure & Tax
S-Corp or LLC? How should you pay yourself? What can you deduct? We answer the questions your CPA might not be raising proactively.
Protection Planning
Disability insurance, life insurance, business overhead coverage — if you get hurt and can’t work, your plan needs to survive it.
