The Practice

Built to serve the people most advisors aren’t built for.

DeMull Financial was founded in 2023 with a specific focus: business owners, tradespeople, professionals, and plan sponsors who needed a financial advisor that understood how they actually earn, operate, and build wealth.

Most financial advisors are great at managing investments. Fewer are built to connect your business finances, tax strategy, retirement design, and personal wealth into a single coordinated plan. That’s what we do.

We operate as a fee-based fiduciary — meaning our compensation is transparent, and we’re legally required to act in your best interest. Not a product we earn a commission on, not a fund company’s preferred list. Just your best interest.

Values
