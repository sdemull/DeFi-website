What We Do

Your business and your
wealth, finally connected.

Running a business creates a financial complexity that most advisors aren’t built to handle. We specialize in connecting your business finances to your personal wealth — entity structure, compensation, taxes, retirement, and exit — as one coordinated strategy.

The Gap We Fill

Most advisors handle either the business or the personal side. We do both.

Business owners have a CPA who files taxes, maybe an attorney who set up the entity, and an investment account somewhere. What they rarely have is someone who connects all of it — who thinks about how your S-Corp salary affects your retirement plan, or how your business exit strategy affects your estate.

That’s the work we do. We sit at the intersection of your business and your personal finances and make sure the decisions in one world optimize the outcome in the other.

Business entity structure analysis — S-Corp, LLC, C-Corp, and hybrid structures
Owner compensation design — salary, distributions, and benefits optimization
Business cash management — how to deploy idle business cash most efficiently
Retirement plan design maximized for the owner — not just the employees
Key person and buy-sell insurance analysis
Business valuation awareness and exit planning integration
What We Address

Every dimension of your financial life as a business owner.

Entity & Structure Strategy
The right business structure can save you thousands annually in taxes — and create serious exposure if it’s wrong. We analyze your current structure, model S-Corp election savings, and make recommendations grounded in your actual income and goals.
Owner Compensation Design
Your W-2 salary affects your self-employment taxes, Social Security benefits, retirement contributions, and loan eligibility. We help you find the right balance between salary and distributions to optimize all of them simultaneously.
Business Cash Strategy
Cash sitting in your business is an opportunity with a cost. We evaluate the most efficient deployment — whether that’s additional retirement contributions, personal distribution, reinvestment, or holding as working capital.
Protection & Risk Management
Disability insurance, life insurance, and buy-sell agreements are often the most neglected areas of business owner planning. We analyze your exposure and identify gaps before they become crises.
Exit & Succession Planning
Whether you plan to sell in 5 years or hand the business to a family member, the decisions you make today affect the outcome dramatically. We integrate exit planning into your financial plan early so you’re not scrambling at the end.
Retirement Plan Design
Your business is one of the most powerful retirement savings vehicles you have. We design a plan — Solo 401(k), Safe Harbor, Defined Benefit — that maximizes what goes in your pocket while satisfying IRS requirements for any employees.
