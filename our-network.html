LPL Financial

DeMull Financial offers securities and advisory services through LPL Financial, one of the nation’s largest independent broker-dealers. LPL provides the regulatory infrastructure, technology, and custodial services that allow independent advisors like Sage DeMull to operate as a fiduciary without being tied to a single product family or proprietary fund list.

Your investment accounts held through LPL are covered by SIPC insurance up to $500,000 per account, and LPL maintains additional coverage through Lloyd’s of London. You can verify Sage’s registration and background at any time through FINRA BrokerCheck.
