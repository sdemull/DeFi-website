Your Responsibility. Our Expertise.

Offering a retirement plan is a benefit.
Managing one is a fiduciary obligation.

When you sponsor a 401(k), you take on legal responsibility for how it’s run. Most business owners don’t realize how much exposure that creates — or how straightforward it is to manage properly with the right support.

We work with plan sponsors across Michigan to keep their plans compliant, cost-effective, and genuinely valuable for their employees.

Our Plan Sponsor Services

We help you run a great plan — and protect yourself doing it

Fiduciary Support
We act as a co-fiduciary on your plan, documenting the process, monitoring investments, and helping you meet your legal obligations as a plan sponsor.
Investment Monitoring
Regular investment reviews against benchmarks — so you can demonstrate a prudent process and make changes when the data supports it.
Fee Benchmarking
We compare your plan costs against the market so you know you’re paying a fair price — and have documentation if you’re ever questioned.
Participant Education
Enrollment meetings, one-on-one guidance, and ongoing support to help your employees actually use the benefit you’re providing.
